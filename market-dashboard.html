What This Means for Physical Buyers

FOB Santos

Coffee — Fine Cup & Sugar

Fine Cup Arabica (Alta Mogiana, screen 16/17/18) and all sugar grades ship FOB Santos. ICE Coffee C and Sugar No.11 are the primary benchmarks — FOB differentials are confirmed after buyer qualification.

FOB Vitória

Coffee — Rio Minas, Conilon & Pepper

Rio Minas Arabica, Conilon Robusta, and ASTA 570+ black pepper ship FOB Vitória. Pepper has no futures benchmark — pricing is assessed from origin quotes and IPC data.

BRL/USD Impact

Brazilian commodity costs are denominated in BRL at origin. A weakening Real reduces FOB USD costs; a strengthening Real increases them. Monitor USD/BRL alongside commodity futures for true landed-cost analysis.

Energy & Freight

Henry Hub natural gas tracks domestic energy costs affecting processing and logistics. Ocean freight from Santos/Vitória to Gulf, East Africa, and Southeast Asian ports runs 25–40 days depending on routing.
