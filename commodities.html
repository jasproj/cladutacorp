Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything buyers need to know before reaching out.

What is ICUMSA 45 sugar and why does it matter?

ICUMSA 45 is the highest grade of white refined cane sugar, with a whiteness rating of 45 RBU. It is the global standard for human consumption, food manufacturing, and confectionery.

What is the difference between ICUMSA 45 and ICUMSA 150?

ICUMSA 45 is fully refined white sugar for direct consumption. ICUMSA 150 (VHP) is raw cane sugar used as industrial input for refineries and beverage manufacturers — less processed, lower cost.

What is your minimum order quantity?

Sugar: 5,000 MT. Coffee and Black Pepper: 1 container each. Contact us to discuss partial loads for qualified buyers.

What ports do you ship from?

Sugar ships from Port Santos, Brazil. Arabica (NY grades) ships from Vitória. Arabica Rio Minas ships from Porto do Rio de Janeiro. Robusta/Conilon ships from Vitória. Black pepper ships from Vitória, Espírito Santo.

What payment terms do you accept?

Standard: 30% at contract signing, 70% at Bill of Lading. Wire transfer. SBLC accepted for orders over 20 containers. DLC not accepted.

Do you offer Halal certified products?

Yes. Halal certification is available upon request for black pepper shipments. Certification cost is passed through to the buyer. Allow 2–3 weeks from order confirmation.

Can I request samples before placing an order?

Yes. Sample requests are available for qualified buyers. Contact our trading desk with your company details and we will arrange.
