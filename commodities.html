Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

The essentials before you qualify.

What are your minimum order quantities?

Sugar: 5,000 MT. Coffee and black pepper: one full container (FCL) each. Coffee grades can be mixed within a single container. Chicken paws: 10 containers (270 MT) minimum.

Which ports do you ship from?

Coffee ships FOB Vitória; Arabica Rio Minas ships FOB Porto do Rio de Janeiro. Sugar ships FOB Santos. Black pepper ships FOB Vitória. Ports are fixed by product.

What payment terms do you accept?

30% at contract signing and 70% against Bill of Lading, by wire transfer. SBLC is considered for 20+ container programs only. DLC is not accepted.

How is pricing determined?

Quotes are issued against prevailing ICE benchmarks; differentials are confirmed after qualification.

Can I get samples?

Yes. Samples are available to qualified buyers, with freight on the buyer.

Is Halal certification available?

Yes, on request for black pepper shipments. Certification cost is passed through to the buyer.

Do you export chicken paws to China?

Yes. Our chicken paws are sourced from GACC-certified Brazilian processing facilities authorized for export to China, Hong Kong, Japan, Malaysia, Indonesia, Vietnam, and select Middle Eastern markets. All shipments include full export documentation.

Is HALAL certification available for chicken paws?

Yes. HALAL certification is available on request for chicken paws shipments. Certification cost is passed through to the buyer.

Do you screen counterparties?

Yes. All counterparties are subject to KYC and sanctions screening before documents are exchanged.
