Project Overview

A Proven Asset with Untapped Potential

The Humble Dome field in Harris County, Texas has produced over 14 BCF of natural gas. Our reactivation program targets 8 wells with known reserves using modern completion techniques.

Prime Location

Harris County, Texas — minutes from Houston's energy infrastructure corridor with direct access to Kinder Morgan gathering system.

Proven Reserves

14+ BCF historical production with engineering reports indicating significant remaining recoverable reserves in multiple pay zones.

Modern Techniques

Multi-stage fracturing, HF acid treatments, and tubing replacement to restore and enhance well productivity.

Fast Revenue

Existing infrastructure means production can come online within months, not years. First revenue expected Day 30 post-completion.
