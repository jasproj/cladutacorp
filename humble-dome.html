Project Overview

A Proven Asset with Untapped Potential

The Humble Salt Dome has produced over 14 BCF. Our reactivation targets wells with known reserves using modern completion techniques.

Prime Location

Harris County, Texas — minutes from Houston's energy corridor. Bounded by Cross Creek, I-69, and Deerbrook Mall. Direct pipeline access.

Proven Reserves

Wilcox A, B, and C sand formations. Engineering reports indicate significant remaining reserves. Bahr #3 subsalt zone untapped.

Infrastructure In Place

Underground flowlines connecting all wells to main pad at Bahr #4. Separators, stock tanks, compressors, and power generators ready.

Immediate Offtake

Gas contracted through Kinder Morgan. Oil trucked by Firebird (Houston) and CDL Crude (Humble). All buyers secured at wellhead.
