The Opportunity

Proven Assets. Modern Techniques. Multiple Exit Channels.

A historic Texas salt dome field with documented production history and significant remaining reserves, available for reactivation using modern completion techniques.

Historic Production

This field has produced over 14 BCF of natural gas across multiple wells. Original production rates exceeded 7,000 MCFD from individual wells.

Proven Reserves

Multiple pay zones in the Wilcox formation remain untapped. Engineering reports indicate significant recoverable reserves using modern fracturing techniques.

Infrastructure In Place

Existing underground flowlines, separators, stock tanks, compressors, and surface facilities dramatically reduce capital requirements.

Immediate Offtake

Existing contracts with major pipeline operators and oil purchasers. Production can flow to market within 30 days of completion.
