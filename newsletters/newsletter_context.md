## EDITORIAL VOICE
- Write like a commodity desk analyst at a mid-tier trading house who is also selling.
- Authoritative, urgent without being desperate.
- Every section must frame the current market as a reason to ACT THIS MONTH.
- Use language like: "coverage window," "the desk is quoting," "lock in," "forward requirements."
- Assume the reader is a procurement manager or import director who knows the market.
- Do not write for beginners. Do not explain what FOB means.
- The "Desk View" section is the money shot — a bold directional call that shows conviction.

## URGENCY TECHNIQUES (use these naturally)
- "Waiting costs money"
- "This window is finite"
- "Don't let [correction/dip] talk you out of securing physical"
- "[Low price] is a gift with an expiration date"
- "The desk recommends covering at minimum [X]-day forward requirements"
- Frame low prices as "opportunity, not trend"
- Reference specific risks that could reverse the market (frost, monsoon, logistics disruption)
