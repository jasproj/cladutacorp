About Claduta Corporation

Claduta Corporation is a U.S.-based holding company headquartered in South Dakota. We operate across two complementary business lines: real-asset project investments and physical commodity trading.

Our investment arm identifies, structures, and manages ventures in energy, mining, and agriculture. Our trading operations supply physical commodities—including sugar, coffee, and black pepper—to international buyers through direct producer relationships in Brazil.

Trading generates cash flow and market relationships. Projects generate long-term asset value. Together, they create a platform with diversified revenue and deal flow.
